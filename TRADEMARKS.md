# Project name and branding

The source license grants rights to use, modify, and redistribute OpenClass code.
It does not grant permission to imply official project affiliation or endorsement.

You may refer truthfully to OpenClass, acknowledge its use, and describe compatibility.
Forks and modified distributions should clearly identify their maintainers and state
that they are unofficial when confusion is possible. Do not use official branding
to misrepresent a fork as an official release or an endorsed product.

Seek project-lead permission for branding that implies sponsorship, endorsement,
or official status. This policy does not prohibit forks or alter AGPL rights.
No registered-trademark status is asserted here. Brand assets, domains, and any
future registration or licensing arrangements require a separate project decision.
