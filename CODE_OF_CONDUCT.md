# Code of Conduct

OpenClass welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat people
with respect, critique ideas constructively, and make collaboration accessible.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, disclosure of private
information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable. This applies to project
spaces and to people representing the project publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may warn, temporarily suspend,
or permanently exclude participants based on severity and repetition. Decisions
should be proportionate, explained where safe, and handled without retaliation.

Report conduct issues privately to the project lead through a private channel listed
on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/yassincodes404). Do not publish another
person's private information in a report. A dedicated conduct contact must be set
before broader community onboarding. Maintainers with a conflict should recuse
where another maintainer is available. Appeals may be sent to the project lead;
OpenClass currently has no independent appeals panel.
